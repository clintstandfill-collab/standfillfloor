Our Legacy

A Family Name
Built on Craftsmanship

In 1947, David Standfill laid his first floor in Tulsa, Oklahoma — and never stopped. What began as a one-man operation founded on precision and pride has grown across three generations into the most trusted name in luxury hardwood flooring in the region.

Each Standfill project carries that unbroken lineage: hands that have shaped thousands of floors, eyes trained to see grain and grain match, and a standard that hasn't wavered in nearly eight decades.

We don't build floors. We build heirlooms.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Q

What types of hardwood flooring does Standfill install?

We install all solid hardwood species including white oak, walnut, cherry, and maple. We specialize in plank flooring as well as custom patterned floors — herringbone, chevron, Versailles parquet, and bespoke medallion inlays for luxury residential homes.

Q

Do you offer floor sanding and refinishing?

Yes. We provide full floor sanding and refinishing using dust-contained equipment. We restore and refinish existing hardwood floors — returning them to bare wood before applying your choice of stain and topcoat finish.

Q

How long has Standfill Floor Company been in business?

Standfill Floor Company was founded in 1947 by David Standfill in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The company has operated continuously for over 78 years and is now led by the third generation of the Standfill family.

Q

Do you specialize in luxury or high-end residential flooring?

Yes. We focus exclusively on luxury residential hardwood flooring. Our clients are discerning homeowners who demand exceptional craftsmanship, premium solid wood species, and custom finishing — not engineered shortcuts or mass-market materials.

Q

What areas of Tulsa and Oklahoma do you serve?

We serve the greater Tulsa metro area including South Tulsa, Midtown, Jenks, Broken Arrow, Owasso, and surrounding communities throughout northeastern Oklahoma.

Q

How do I request a quote or consultation?

Every project begins with a complimentary in-home consultation. Call us at (918) 555-0100 or email hello@standfillfloors.com. A member of the Standfill team will be in touch within one business day to schedule your visit.

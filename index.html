Our Legacy

A Family Name
Built on Craftsmanship

In 1947, L.A. Standfill laid his first floor in Little Rock, Arkansas—and that's where it all started. What began as a one-man operation built on hard work and attention to detail has been passed down through three generations.

Today, every Standfill project reflects that history. We've worked on thousands of floors over the years, and we still take the same pride in getting the details right—matching grain, delivering a clean finish, and doing the job the way it should be done. That standard hasn't changed.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Q

What types of hardwood flooring does Standfill install?

We install all solid hardwood species including white oak, walnut, cherry, and maple. We specialize in plank flooring as well as custom patterned floors — herringbone, chevron, Versailles parquet, and bespoke medallion inlays for luxury residential homes.

Q

Do you offer floor sanding and refinishing?

Yes. We provide full floor sanding and refinishing using dust-contained equipment. We restore and refinish existing hardwood floors — returning them to bare wood before applying your choice of stain and topcoat finish.

Q

How long has Standfill Floor Company been in business?

Standfill Floor Company was founded in 1947 by L.A.Standfill in Little Rock, Arkansas. The company has operated continuously for over 78 years and is now led by the second and third generation of the Standfill family.

Q

Do you specialize in luxury or high-end residential flooring?

Yes. We focus exclusively on luxury residential and commercial hardwood flooring. Our clients are discerning homeowners who demand exceptional craftsmanship, premium solid wood species, and custom finishing — not engineered shortcuts or mass-market materials.

Q

What areas of Little Rock and Arkansas do you serve?

We serve the greater Little Rock metro area including West Little Rock, the Heights, Chenal, Conway, Benton, Bryant, Maumelle, and surrounding communities throughout central Arkansas.

Q

How do I request a quote or consultation?

Every project begins with a complimentary in-home consultation. Call us at (501) 847-0393 or email standfillfloors@gmail.com. A member of the Standfill team will be in touch to schedule your visit.
